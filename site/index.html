The Psychopath Whisperer

Why do psychopaths behave the way they do? Kent Kiehl has spent three decades interviewing people with psychopathic traits and scanning their brains with a mobile MRI he takes into prisons. This site follows that science: the book, the news, and the most influential research and researchers in the field.

From the laboratory of Kent A. Kiehl
