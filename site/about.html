About this site

A place to review and discuss the latest scientific research on psychopathy.

The Psychopath Whisperer website was created to review and discuss the latest scientific research on psychopathy. Our hope is that it will improve understanding, and correct misconceptions, of those few among us who lack empathy and a conscience, on the basis of scientific data.

The website is managed by scientists in the laboratory of Dr. Kent A. Kiehl (kentkiehl.com). We welcome feedback: if you have questions or comments, please email psychopath.whisperer@gmail.com.

Kent A. Kiehl, PhD

Kent Kiehl is Executive Science Officer and Director of Mobile Imaging at the nonprofit Mind Research Network and Professor of Psychology, Neurosciences and Law at the University of New Mexico in Albuquerque. He has studied psychopathy for more than 30 years and has published more than 280 peer-reviewed papers. He is the author of The Psychopath Whisperer: The Science of Those Without Conscience (Crown, 2014) and co-editor of the Handbook on Psychopathy and Law (Oxford University Press, 2013).

Kiehl trained with Robert Hare, author of the Psychopathy Checklist-Revised, and Peter Liddle at the University of British Columbia, where he published the first fMRI study of brain dysfunction in criminal psychopaths. Because no prison in the United States has an MRI scanner, he designed a mobile MRI system that brings the scanner to correctional and forensic facilities. It has scanned more than 6,000 incarcerated people across a dozen states, creating the largest forensic neuroimaging repository in the world.

In 2026 he received the Robert D. Hare Lifetime Achievement Award, the highest honor of the Society for the Scientific Study of Psychopathy.
